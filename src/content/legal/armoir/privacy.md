## Summary

Armoir is a wardrobe and outfit app. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. We don't run servers that receive your data, and we don't collect, see, or sell anything you put into the app.

Your wardrobe lives on your iPhone. If iCloud is on, it also syncs to your own private iCloud database, which only you can see. A few features ask Apple, or a store website you choose, for information. Those are listed below, along with exactly what they send.

## What stays on your iPhone

- **Your wardrobe.** Your items, outfits, wear history, planned outfits, trips, packing lists, and style quiz answers are kept in the app's database on your iPhone.
- **Photos.** When you take or choose a photo of a piece of clothing, Armoir removes the background and suggests tags (type, color, and so on) on your iPhone using Apple's Vision framework. The photo isn't uploaded anywhere. Armoir keeps the cut-out image and a small thumbnail, not the original photo.
- **Outfit explanations.** On iPhones that support Apple Intelligence, the short "why this works" text for an outfit is written by Apple's on-device language model. Otherwise Armoir uses built-in templates. Either way, this happens on your iPhone.
- **Calendar events.** If you allow calendar access, Armoir reads upcoming events on your iPhone to guess how dressy a day is (for example, "Team lunch" reads as smart-casual). Armoir never adds, changes, or deletes events, and it doesn't save event names or details. It only saves the kind of occasion (such as "work") with an outfit you plan for that day.
- **Notifications.** Morning outfit reminders and evening event reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. Event reminders include the event's name and time. Nothing is sent through a push service to deliver them.
- **Settings.** Your settings are stored on your iPhone and don't sync. These include notification times, temperature units, appearance, insight preferences, and the city you picked for weather.

## iCloud sync

If you're signed in to iCloud, Armoir syncs your wardrobe data through Apple's CloudKit to the private database in your own iCloud account. This data includes items with their images, outfits, wear history, trips (including destination names and coordinates), packing lists, and your style profile. This keeps your devices in step. Only you can see that database. We can't read it.

If iCloud isn't available, Armoir works the same way, and your data stays on this iPhone only. You can see whether sync is on in Settings. Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Services Armoir uses

### Apple Weather

To suggest outfits for the weather, Armoir sends a location to Apple's WeatherKit service. That location is either your current location or a city you picked. Trip forecasts send the trip destination. Armoir keeps the weather results in memory for about 30 minutes and doesn't save your current location. A city you pick is saved in the app's settings on your iPhone, and a trip's destination is saved with the trip.

### Apple Maps (city names and search)

When you use your current location, Armoir asks Apple Maps for the name of the city you're in. When you search for a city for weather or a trip, your search text is sent to Apple Maps to find matches.

### App Store

Armoir Plus is an optional subscription sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment, and we never see your payment details. Armoir asks the App Store which subscriptions are active on your Apple Account so it can unlock Plus features.

### Stores you paste links from

When you paste a product link to add an item, your iPhone connects directly to that store's website. It loads the page to read the product's name and details, then downloads the product photo. Like any web browser, this request includes your iPhone's IP address, a standard browser identifier, and your preferred languages. If the store doesn't answer that request, Armoir opens the page in a hidden, private browser window instead, which runs the store's own page scripts the way Safari would. Either way, Armoir doesn't send any of your wardrobe data, starts with no cookies, and discards any cookies or site data as soon as the page has been read. The link is saved with the item so you can open it later. Each store's own privacy policy applies to its website.

## Permissions

Armoir asks for these only when you use the feature that needs them. You can change any of them later in the iPhone Settings app.

- **Camera.** Used to photograph clothing.
- **Photos.** Used to pick photos of clothing you already have.
- **Location (While Using).** Used to check local weather. Armoir asks for approximate location by default, which is all it needs; you can leave Precise Location off. If you'd rather not share your location, you can pick a city instead.
- **Calendar.** Used to read upcoming events so outfits can suit your day. This is read-only.
- **Notifications.** Used for the morning outfit reminder and the evening-before-an-event reminder.

## Sharing

When you share an outfit, Armoir creates an image and opens the standard iOS share sheet. Nothing is shared unless you choose where to send it.

## Children

Armoir isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone. Because the app sends us nothing, we hold no data about any user.

## Deleting your data

To erase everything, go to Settings and tap **Delete all my data**. This removes your items, outfits, wear history, trips, packing lists, and style profile from your iPhone. It also clears scheduled reminders and resets your settings. If iCloud sync is on, the deletions also sync to your iCloud database. This finishes in the background and can complete the next time you open the app. Deleting your data doesn't cancel an Armoir Plus subscription. To cancel, use your Apple Account's subscription settings.

You can also delete the app. Removing it deletes the data on your iPhone, but not any copy in iCloud. To remove that copy, use **Delete all my data** first, or manage Armoir's data in your iPhone's iCloud settings.

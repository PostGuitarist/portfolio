Ebbline does not collect any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking, and the app contains no third-party SDKs.

## Where your data is kept

- **Your events and checkpoints** are stored on your iPhone or iPad and, if you use iCloud, in your private iCloud database (see iCloud below). Ebbline's widgets read a copy of them from storage on the same device.
- **Reminders** are local notifications, scheduled and delivered on your device. Nothing is sent to a server.

## Calendar and Contacts

If you choose to import, Ebbline asks for access to your Calendar or Contacts. It reads them on your device only to show you a list to pick from: upcoming events, or contacts' names and birthdays. Only the items you pick are saved, as Ebbline events, and they sync like any other event (see iCloud below). With Ebbline Pro, a countdown imported from Calendar stays linked to its event: while Calendar access is on, Ebbline checks that event on your device and moves the countdown if the event's date or title changes. Ebbline never writes to your Calendar or Contacts, and nothing from them is sent to the developer or anyone else. You can turn access off at any time in Settings › Ebbline.

## iCloud

If you're signed in to iCloud, Ebbline syncs your events through your **private iCloud database**, so they appear on your other devices. That data is stored by Apple under your Apple Account. The developer can't see it, read it, or access it. You can turn this off in Settings › [your name] › iCloud.

## Sharing

If you share an event, Ebbline puts that event (its title, dates, look, note and checkpoints) in your iCloud and shares it with the people you invite, using Apple's iCloud sharing. They see it, and can change it if you allow. Your reminders and your other events aren't shared. Shared events are stored by Apple under your and their Apple Accounts; the developer can't see them. You can stop sharing at any time, and anyone you share with can leave.

## Purchases

Ebbline Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple processes the payment. The developer receives no payment details, and nothing that identifies you.

## Children

Ebbline doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.

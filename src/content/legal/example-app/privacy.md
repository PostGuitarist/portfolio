This Privacy Policy explains how **Example App** ("the App", "we", "us") handles your information. By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

## Summary

- The App does not require an account.
- We do not sell your personal information.
- Data you create in the App is stored on your device and, if you enable it, in your private iCloud account.

## Information We Collect

### Information you provide

Content you create in the App (for example, notes or settings) is stored locally on your device. If iCloud sync is enabled, it is stored in your private iCloud database, which we cannot access.

### Information collected automatically

The App may collect anonymous, aggregated diagnostics (such as crash reports) through Apple's App Store Connect if you have opted in to share analytics with app developers in your device settings. This data does not identify you personally.

## How We Use Information

We use the limited information available to us to:

- Operate, maintain, and improve the App
- Diagnose crashes and fix bugs
- Respond to support requests you send us

## Third-Party Services

The App may use the following Apple services, which are governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/):

- iCloud / CloudKit (data sync)
- StoreKit (in-app purchases, if applicable)

## Data Retention and Deletion

Data stored on your device is removed when you delete the App. Data stored in iCloud can be removed in **Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage**. If you have contacted us by email, you can ask us to delete that correspondence at any time.

## Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

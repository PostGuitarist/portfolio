## Summary

Myotrx is a training and nutrition app. You sign in with Apple, and your workouts, programs, and nutrition logs are synced through our servers so they're on your iPhone and Apple Watch. We use that data only to run the app for you. We don't sell it, we don't show ads, and we don't use third-party analytics or tracking.

Meal photos are analyzed on your iPhone and never uploaded. Data from Apple Health is never used for advertising or shared with anyone.

## Your account

Myotrx uses **Sign in with Apple**. When you sign in, Apple gives us an identifier for your account that's unique to Myotrx. If you choose to share them, Apple also gives us your name and email address. You can choose **Hide My Email**, in which case we receive an Apple relay address that forwards to you. We use your email only to contact you about your account. We never use it for marketing unless you ask us to.

## What we store

To sync your data between your devices, we store the following with your account:

- **Training.** Your programs (including ones you build yourself), workout sessions, and every set you log: exercise, reps, weight, rest time, set type (such as drop sets or supersets), and personal records. We also store the progression suggestions calculated from your history.
- **Onboarding answers.** Your goal, experience level, available equipment, and how many days a week you train, used to recommend a program.
- **Nutrition.** The foods, calories, and macros you log.
- **Body weight.** The weights you enter or allow Myotrx to read from Apple Health, used to show your progress and to record your body weight with a workout.
- **Your subscription status.** See "Purchases" below.

The app keeps a copy of this data on your iPhone and Apple Watch, so you can log a workout without a signal. Changes sync when you're back online.

## What stays on your devices

- **Meal photos.** When you photograph a meal, Myotrx estimates what's in it on your iPhone using Apple's Vision and Core ML. The photo isn't uploaded anywhere. Only the entry you confirm, the food and its calories and macros, is saved and synced.
- **Settings.** Your app preferences are stored on your device.
- **Sign-in tokens.** The tokens that keep you signed in are stored in your device's Keychain.

## Apple Health

Myotrx works with Apple Health only if you allow it, and you choose which data types it can use.

- **Writes:** completed workouts and the active energy you burned, and body weight you enter in Myotrx.
- **Reads:** body weight.

Data that Myotrx reads from Apple Health is used only to provide features in the app. It's never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never shared with anyone. You can change Myotrx's access at any time in the iPhone Settings app, under Health → Data Access & Devices.

## Apple Watch

The Myotrx Apple Watch app logs workouts on your wrist. It syncs with your iPhone when it's nearby, and with our servers on its own when it isn't, using the same account.

## Purchases

Myotrx subscriptions are sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment, and we never see your payment details. To unlock subscriber content, our servers receive notifications from Apple about your subscription: the product, its status, its renewal and expiry dates, and Apple's transaction identifiers. We link these to your account so your subscription works on all your devices.

## Who processes your data

- **Our hosting provider** runs our servers and database on our behalf. It stores your data only to provide that service to us and isn't allowed to use it for anything else.
- **Apple** provides Sign in with Apple, Apple Health, the App Store, and push notifications, under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

We don't share your data with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

The exercise library and curated programs are downloaded from our servers and cached on your device. Those downloads don't include any of your personal data.

## Crash reports

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. These don't identify you. Myotrx doesn't include any third-party analytics or crash-reporting tools.

## Sharing

When you share a workout summary, Myotrx creates an image and opens the standard iOS share sheet. Nothing is shared unless you choose where to send it. Myotrx has no social features inside the app, so no one else can see your data.

## Security

Data between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit with TLS. Access to your data requires your account's sign-in tokens.

## Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data for as long as you have an account.

To delete your account, go to Settings → **Delete account**. This deletes your account and all of your data from our servers, and signs you out on every device. Copies in our backups are deleted when those backups expire. Data Myotrx already wrote to Apple Health stays there until you delete it in the Health app.

Deleting your account doesn't cancel a subscription. To cancel, use your Apple Account's subscription settings. You can also stop Myotrx from using your Apple ID in the iPhone Settings app, under your name → Sign in with Apple.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete the personal data we hold about you, and to object to how we use it. You can delete your data in the app at any time. For anything else, email us and we'll respond within 30 days. We don't sell or share personal data for targeted advertising.

## Children

Myotrx isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top. If a change affects what data we collect or how we use it, we'll tell you in the app before it applies.
